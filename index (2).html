      About rUNta

      The Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá campus, is one of the largest university campuses in Colombia and South America. Because of its size, students, teachers, staff, and visitors may have difficulty finding buildings, facilities, and services. Sometimes, even students do not know where all the places on campus are located.

      Our project aims to solve this problem by developing an application that helps people navigate the university more easily. The main users will be students, but teachers, university staff, visitors, and anyone who visits the campus will also benefit from the app.

      The main function of our application is to help users locate buildings, stationery stores, and other facilities on campus. This feature will help people find specific places, save time, and make their visits easier. The most important function is the location feature because it allows users to find places they do not know and navigate the campus more efficiently.

      Finally, the application will require an internet connection to work properly and provide accurate location information. Overall, our goal is to make the Universidad Nacional de Colombia campus easier to explore and help people find the places and services they need.

      How to use it
      • No account or registration is needed. Just open the page.
      • Tap a category button to see its pins. Tap a pin or a place in the list to see it in Google Maps.
      • Choose a starting point and a destination to see the walking route.
      • An internet connection is required.
